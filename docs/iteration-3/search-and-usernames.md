## B. Usernames (contract §2)

**Rules (client mirrors the server).** Optional; `null` until set; never generated, pre-filled or derived from the email. Typing lowercases the text and drops a leading "@"; saving trims. Valid = 3–20 characters of `a-z 0-9 _` (`^[a-z0-9_]{3,20}$`). Client message, identical to the server's `fields.username`: **"3-20 characters, letters, numbers, and underscores only."** One extra client-only check, for privacy (the UF email stays hidden until two people team up): the username may not equal the part of the viewer's own UF email before the @ → **"Don't use the part of your UF email before the @. Your email stays private until you team up."** (listed for Luka in F).

**Field, Profile setup step 2 "The basics"**, directly after Name:
- Label "Username" + muted "(optional)". A muted "@" sits inside the input on the left (`aria-hidden`).
- Help (says it is public before they type, and why not to reuse the email name): "Public: shown on your card, and classmates can search for it. Pick something other than your UF email name, which stays private. 3-20 lowercase letters, numbers or underscores."
- Typing that changes the text (lowercasing, a dropped "@") puts the caret back in a layout effect right after React commits, so fast typing never lands letters in the middle.
- `autoComplete="off"` (never "username": browsers would offer the UF email), `autoCapitalize="none"`, `spellCheck={false}`, `maxLength` 30 (a long paste gets the message instead of being cut silently).
- Errors follow the Field idiom: shown after the first Finish, then live; `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`; focus goes to the field. Draft and Preview card include the username.

**Saving.** `PUT /api/me` carries `username` only when usernames are on **and** the value changed: a new value → the string; a field emptied that had a value → `null`; unchanged or still empty → no key at all. Server answers:
- `409 username_taken` → show `fields.username` (contract: "That username is taken."; use that text if the field is missing).
- `400 validation` with `fields.username` → show it.
- Both: message under the field, focus there, bar alert "Your profile wasn't saved. Fix the username above and press Finish again." Every other answer is kept and the draft is saved as today.
- The same for `fields.name`, `fields.major` and `fields.year` (under the field; the bar names them all, e.g. "Fix the major above and press Finish again."). Any other `fields.<key>` is named in the bar: "Your profile wasn't saved. Pace: {message} Your answers are still here."

**Major (same card, fix round 1).** Required (`*`). The server takes only the four majors, and a new account (or an old one) may have `""`: the select then shows a disabled "Choose your major" instead of showing "Computer Science" while sending `""`, and Finish says "Pick your major." under it (focus, `aria-invalid`).

**@handles** render as `@priya_builds`: tiny `--color-muted` text, `translate="no"`, own line, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`; nothing (no empty line) when usernames are off or the student has none. Shown on: Find teammates cards (under the name), profile page (under the h1), expanded compare tray column heads, My team slots (also "You" when set), Inbox request and sent-invite rows, the Profile setup Preview card.

**Feature detect.** `usernamesOn = 'username' in viewer` from the latest `GET /api/me`, `PUT /api/me` or verify payload. Off (no key): no field, no `username` in any body, no handles, search placeholders without "@username". Mock: on.

## C. Search (contract §1 and §2 "Search")

**Rules, shared by mock, client safety net and UI** (`lib/search.ts`):
- Normalize: trim, collapse inner spaces, drop one leading "@". Under 2 characters counts as empty: no request, default list, hint "Type at least 2 characters.". Field `maxLength` 50.
- People (`GET /api/students?q=`): the whole query is a case-insensitive substring of `name` or `username`. Nothing else is matched (not skills, major or email; no accent folding, as on the server). All students except the viewer, also those not looking for a team. No limit, no ranking.
- Teams (`GET /api/team-posts?q=`): `project` contains the query (case-insensitive), **or** `course` equals the query with all spaces removed (case-insensitive). If the query with spaces removed looks like a course code (`^[A-Za-z]{3}\d{4}[A-Za-z]?$`), the frontend **sends** it compact and uppercase ("cis 4301" → `q=CIS4301`), so a server doing an exact match still finds it. Members' names are not searched.
- `api.searchStudents` / `api.searchTeamPosts` in `src/api/index.ts` (`encodeURIComponent`) filter the answer again with the same matcher, so a server that ignores `q` or matches more (LIKE treats `_` as a wildcard) still shows correct results.
- Keep from the partial work: 250 ms debounce, Enter searches at once (no reload), the same query is never sent twice in a row, only the newest answer applies, query kept in AppData (not the URL) across profile visits and project switches, cleared on sign-out. With a query, filters mark instead of hide: fits first, then the rest with a note, then (people) your current teammates; the Sort menu applies inside each group; ↺ resets filters only. Remove the 20-result cap and the "Showing the first 20 matches" line.

**/find, "Search all students"** (above the filter bar, `<form role="search" aria-label="Search students">`).
- Placeholder "Name or @username" (usernames off: "Name"). Hint (`aria-describedby`): "Finds anyone with a profile by name or @username, including people not looking for a team." (off: "…by name, including…").
- Card notes: "Not looking for a team right now. You can still invite them." (Invite stays enabled) · "Outside your filters: Role, Shared time, Goal" (only the failing ones) · current teammates get the note "On your team for {project}." (not a chip among the skills) and "Already on {project}" instead of Invite, with no Compare box. Results not yet loaded are added to AppData `students`, so View profile, Compare and Invite work.
- Count (existing polite `.results-meta`, set once per finished search). "Fit" means looking, not on your team and passing every filter, so the count says so: "{n} student(s) found for “{q}” · {k} are looking and fit your filters" / "· 1 is looking and fits your filters" / "· none are looking and fit your filters".
- Loading: "Searching…" (tiny muted, not live) beside the label; list `aria-busy="true"`, cards at 60 % opacity, no animation.
- Empty: heading "No one matches “{q}”", text "Search looks at names and @usernames, not skills or majors. Check the spelling." (off: "Search looks at names, not skills…"), button "Clear search".
- Error: heading "Search isn't working right now", the error message (e.g. "Couldn't reach the server."), buttons "Try again" and "Clear search"; count "Search failed.".

**/teams, "Search open teams"** (`aria-label="Search teams"`). Placeholder "Project name or course code". Hint: "Type a project name, or a full course code such as CIS4301. Only teams with open spots show, never one you're in." Notes "Outside your filters: Roles, Goal, Shared time". Count "{n} team(s) found for “{q}” · {k} fit your filters". Empty: "No teams match “{q}”", text "Search looks at project names and full course codes, such as CIS4301 or CIS 4301.", "Clear search". Loading and error as on /find. Request to join and Cancel request work on results.

**Keyboard and accessibility.** Escape in a filled field clears it and keeps focus (empty field: nothing). Every Clear search control returns focus to the field. × button `aria-label="Clear search"`, 44 × 44 px; WebKit's native cancel button hidden. Tab: field, ×, filter bar. Focus ring = `.setup-input` style in all four looks; text AA, field border ≥ 3:1. **390 px:** field full width, 44 px tall, 16 px text; hint, count and empty state wrap; no horizontal scroll in any look.
